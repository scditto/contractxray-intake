Effective Date: February 28, 2026

1. Who We Are

Contract X-Ray is a service of Nautilus Health Institute, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to democratizing access to high-performance health plans. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our contract analysis service.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit a contract for analysis, we collect: contact information (email, company name), contract details (PBM name), uploaded documents (PDF), and for Premium, payment processed securely through Stripe. We do not store your credit card details.

3. How We Use Your Information

Solely to generate your analysis report, deliver it to your email, respond to questions, and improve our scoring methodology using anonymized aggregated data.

4. Who Has Access to Your Contract

Access is strictly limited to Nautilus analysts and secure AI systems used in the analysis process. We do not sell, share, or disclose your contract to third parties, PBMs, competitors, or any external organizations.

Contracts and analysis are retained for 12 months to support follow-up questions. After this period, contracts are securely deleted unless you request otherwise.

Essential: Quick Look Report

One question answered: does this contract warrant a deeper look?

The Quick Look is a triage tool, not a full evaluation. It reads your contract against fiduciary-aligned standards and gives you a fast directional read — so you know whether to act before committing to a full analysis.

  • Every provision rated Good, Concern, or Red Flag — no scoring complexity, just a clear signal
  • Key findings summary: top strengths and priority concerns
  • CAA 2026 alignment assessment
  • Delivered as a PDF you can share immediately

Methodology

Your contract is evaluated against 35 issues across 10 key provisions, including fiduciary acknowledgment, pricing transparency, audit rights, and data ownership. Ratings are based solely on explicit contract language — not marketing claims or business reputation.

⏱ PDF delivered in under 1 hour

Premium: Full Analysis Suite

The complete fiduciary picture — condensed for action.

The Premium suite gives you everything you need to evaluate, negotiate, and communicate your contract's gaps — built for the people who have to do something about it.

Scorecard

A complete evaluation condensed to a single page. Shows the overall 0–100 score, provision-by-provision breakdown with color-coded ratings, and key findings. This is what you bring to the benefits committee or the board meeting.

  • Overall score with full provision breakdown
  • Issue-by-issue analysis across all 35 criteria
  • Evidence citations referencing your specific contract language
  • CAA 2026 compliance assessment

Negotiation Guide

A full action plan for brokers and attorneys working to improve the contract. Built around the specific gaps identified in your Scorecard.

  • Gap Analysis: provision-level findings with risk assessment
  • Model Contract Language: suggested replacement text for each gap
  • Negotiation Talking Points: framing for PBM conversations

Executive Brief

A plain-language summary for stakeholders who need to be informed but may not be conversant in PBM contract details. Designed for board members, senior leadership, and plan sponsors who need the bottom line without the technical depth.
